# RTerm for Forward Deployed Engineers — The FDE Playbook

> The FDE Playbook is the official field guide for using RTerm as a Forward Deployed Engineer's primary workstation. RTerm is an AI-native terminal and agentic-AI operations platform built around the four-layer FDE mental model: Reach (SSH/WinRM/serial into any estate), Command (parallel fleet execution), Agent (AI-driven observe→reason→act under policy), and Operations (MOP-gated changes, SRE observability, leave-behind automation). This site covers the operating rhythm, worked field scenarios, and the day-in-the-life of an FDE running RTerm.

## What a Forward Deployed Engineer does

A Forward Deployed Engineer works inside customer production environments — untangling outages at 02:00, landing migrations with zero downtime, and onboarding onto undocumented estates. The FDE operating rhythm has five moves:

1. **Set the stage** — name the environment, blast radius, and clock
2. **Gather facts** — pull ground truth from every host in parallel; never guess
3. **Track the issue** — form a hypothesis and drive it with tool calls
4. **Resolve under guardrails** — change production safely, prove it worked
5. **Lock it in** — automate the recurrence so it never pages anyone again
